    What should your bot do?

    Each round buys ETH and attempts to close it after your holding time. The bot waits when your rules are not met. This is a rules-based test, not a price prediction.

    Up to 0.004 ETH per order. The market minimum is checked before starting.

    Adds up every order, including closes. This is not a loss limit.

    At least 0.12 STT. The bot also keeps 1 STT untouched in its fee balance.

    After this, it stops opening new rounds.

    An exit can take longer or fail. Follow its activity.

    Price rules and safety details

    ETH-PERP on Tokyo testnet. The bot waits if the spread exceeds 0.05%, allows at most 0.10% price movement from the relevant reference, and uses at most 25% of visible depth.

    For rounds, it stops new entries and attempts to close if account equity falls by 0.25 test USDso or below 1.5 test USDso. These are triggers, not guaranteed loss limits. The exit needs working network access, permissions, liquidity and fees.

    Limits are cumulative. Restarting never renews them. Your Link a Bot grant can cover more markets than this ETH-only plan; these limits are enforced by the local software.
